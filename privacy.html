Lamp & Path: Privacy Policy

Effective: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Lamp & Path is a Bible study app for Android and Windows, published by [YOUR LLC NAME]. We built it to be simple and private.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you to us or anyone else.

Your notes and progress

Your reading progress, journal entries and memory verses are stored only on your own device. We never see them and cannot access them. On Android, if backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, which Google's privacy policy covers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information from your device.

Hosting

On Windows, the app's files are delivered by our web host, GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address, when the app downloads or updates. We do not receive or use this information.

Links to other websites

The “Read online” button opens Bible Gateway (biblegateway.com) in your web browser. That website has its own privacy policy, which applies when you visit it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
